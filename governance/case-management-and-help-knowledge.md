# Case management and help knowledge

## Operational cases

Use [Software Issue Reporting Format](../templates/operations/software-issue-reporting.md) for reporters and [Case Classification Guide](../templates/operations/case-classification-guide.md) for investigators. They are operational templates, separate from PRD, 2A and FARS. No case-status lifecycle is introduced. Existing product permissions and Zoho CRM procedures remain applicable; this policy does not configure Zoho.

Report observable facts without requiring the reporter to diagnose the cause. Classify from investigation and approved requirements, observed behaviour and evidence. A complaint is not automatically a bug, and the team implementing a correction did not necessarily cause the problem. Staff negligence requires evidence of accessible, adequately communicated instructions; misunderstanding alone is insufficient.

Link process conflicts to the relevant 2A decision. Propose FARS updates for recurring failure or frustration, with human review before adding agent contributions. A case or classification does not authorize implementation, disciplinary action, customer communication or automatic case closure. Duplicate handling retains additional evidence and follows the product's authorized closure process.

## CPI as a knowledge source

CPI can support a help centre through three distinct layers: controlled product records, approved audience-specific articles, and a separately authorized website or assistant. This describes a possible capability, not a deployed help service or permission to build one.

Internal staff guidance may explain approved processes, responsibilities, case classification and verified release state. Customer and guest content must be specifically approved for those audiences. Enforce access controls at retrieval and publication boundaries; hiding an internal link is not access control. Never expose internal investigations, unapproved proposals, credentials, private customer records or restricted governance through public answers.

Articles record an owner, source document IDs and versions, audience, approval, review date and verified availability scope. Publication depends on confirmed customer availability, not a merged branch. Record uncertainty and route unanswered questions to an authorized owner instead of inventing guidance. Refresh affected articles when approved rules or actual availability change. Case trends may reveal poor design or a process defect, not just missing training.

Follow existing [Ada support capability](../rules/ada-support-capability.md), knowledge-training approval and [feature availability](feature-availability.md) rules. Product-specific implementation, credentials and integrations require a separate proposal.
