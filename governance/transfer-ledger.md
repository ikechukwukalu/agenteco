## Conflict precedence

When imported specialist language conflicts with the Operating Constitution, Session Role Selection and Lock, Global Engineering Rules, or Delivery and Pull Request Governance, those canonical Eco Space documents take precedence.

The following AgentHQ behaviours are intentionally not inherited:

- permanent Engineering Manager activation;
- mandatory delegation before specialist work;
- automatic multi-agent participation;
- mandatory delegation reports and active-agent tables;
- repeated loading of the entire context estate;
- large authorization ceremonies beyond the documented standalone command;
- compulsory QA, UI/UX, security, or other reviews without human approval;
- any AI authority to merge a pull request.
