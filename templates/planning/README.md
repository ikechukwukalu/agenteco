# Planning templates

Use [PRD](prd.md) for the request, [2A](2a.md) for agreed actors, actions and flows, and [FARS](fars.md) for per-action risk review. Follow [Requirements and process planning](../../governance/requirements-and-process-planning.md). These are reusable forms with blank cells, numbered rows and separate stable references. Duplicate actor, action and success-point blocks as needed; completed documents have no fixed page limit.

| Content template | Word visual master | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PRD](prd.md) | [PRD Word](prd.docx) | A4 portrait |
| [2A](2a.md) | [2A Word](2a.docx) | A4 portrait |
| [FARS](fars.md) | [FARS Word](fars.docx) | A4 landscape; separate illustrative example |

The supplied Word masters preserve the reviewed layout: black headings, light table headers, readable spacing, numbered rows and blank entries rather than bracketed filler. Markdown records carry authoritative product content; Word files are visual masters or labelled exports, not separate sources of business truth. The masters are the baseline layout, not pre-approved product requirements.

For Word delivery, populate a copy of the corresponding master with the current Markdown content, preserving section order and table structure. Add any governance-required metadata missing from a master, including 2A version and contribution notes in FARS. The 2A Word master includes its existing actor-confirmation block. Keep actual design links clickable, label source version/date and separate examples from completed entries. Render and visually inspect every page before delivery; fix overflow or clipping rather than shrinking text to force a page count. Markdown alone cannot guarantee identical Word rendering. When template content changes, reconcile the master during the next authorized template/export update and disclose any remaining mismatch.

The compact planning FARS is distinct from the existing [engineering FARS evidence record](../product-context/feature-paths-and-recovery.md). Link the records by stable finding IDs; the evidence record retains technical tests, recovery limits and review outcomes without burdening the business form. Do not duplicate conflicting business decisions.

For 2A Word and PDF exports, preserve the approved section order, table columns, references and visual styling. Populate fields and repeat designated blocks without redesigning or omitting sections. The Permissions for actions catalogue does not prescribe roles or assignments. Internal helpful links must never be disclosed to customers or guests, including by assistants; external helpful links must be approved for that audience. Withhold links when audience authorization is uncertain.
