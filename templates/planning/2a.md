## Preparing the linked FARS document

FARS is a separate document derived from this 2A. Record its owner, dates, version, process reference and link to this document. Review every action, requirement, dependency and handoff, including optional actions.


Use the linked simple FARS template: one table per action with a finding reference, possible failure or frustration, avoidance, recovery and verification. Record test evidence when available; do not imply that proposed measures have been implemented.

## Review and maintenance

Walk through the journey with business, design, engineering and QA before implementation.

Before changing a process, review connected processes and linked FARS findings. Distinguish confirmed conflicts, possible impacts and missing information. “No conflict found” applies only to the reviewed scope.

Update affected 2A sections, FARS findings and tests when behaviour changes. Keep engineering detail separate, but bring back constraints that change agreed business behaviour.

## Reference and history rules

Retain references for actions whose identity is unchanged. Assign new references to genuinely new actions. Retire removed references; never reuse them for different actions. Update linked FARS findings and checks when the flow changes.

Revise this 2A for changes to the same process. Create a linked 2A for a genuinely separate process. Preserve earlier approved versions in version-controlled history and keep their links accessible.
